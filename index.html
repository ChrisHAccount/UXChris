About Me

Hi, I'm Chris - a UX Researcher & Designer focused on product discovery and human‑centred retail experiences.

I study how customers make decisions online and how PDPs can replace the confidence people gain through touch.

I'm completing a PhD on tactile decision-making in e-commerce at @UoW. Before moving into UX, I spent 17 years teaching photography, media, web design, and analytics - experience that shaped my ability to communicate clearly, understand user behaviour, and design with purpose.

This portfolio highlights my UX research and design practice.
